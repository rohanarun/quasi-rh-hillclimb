Write P, t₀, b=2t₀/3, δ, p, k and R as in [the long-block proof](long-blocks.md). Put m=t₀/3, λ=10⁻⁶ and η=10⁻⁷. **Conclusion.** Outside O(U^(R−η)) rows, each retained row in a fixed bin admits two inverse factors of lengths x₁,x₂ and two ordinary factors of lengths y₁,y₂ satisfying

    b−9λ < x₁,x₂ < b+λ,      m−9λ < y₁,y₂ < m+λ,
    x₁+x₂+y₁+y₂ ≥ 2t₀−5λ−O(1/log U).

Each normalized squared magnitude is at least U^(δ times its length minus an arbitrarily small prescribed loss), up to a fixed positive constant. All four factors retain the same zero-extended character presentation. The two inverses and the inner ordinary factor share a norm-twist height, which can differ from the outer ordinary factor’s height by the additional common Fourier shift. The original buffered zero and the presentation supplying it may be fixed before either detector cutoff. The outer ordinary factor and the outer inverse begin at the same height. A count of the complementary rows allows this statement to be applied also to any particular original witness fibre.

**Single-factor counts.** Rows with a saturated inverse in [b+λ,t₀−λ], or a saturated ordinary factor of length at least m+λ, have exponent at most R−η for each of the common profile families arising in this proof. **Second detector.** Use cutoff T=2t₀−5λ, which is below 2.26 uniformly. The original Y*=U²⁰ works up to this cutoff, since −3+2.26(5/4−.51)<−1 in the Gamma-line bound and the original height cost is an arbitrarily small fixed power. The resulting saturated inverse of length r and outer ordinary factor of length y₂ satisfy r≤T+O(1/log U), r+y₂≥T−O(1/log U). Remove the rows on which y₂≥m+λ at the preceding count. Now r≥2t₀−m−6λ−O(1/log U).

Set A=U^(t₀−2λ). The fixed annular support at length r is strictly between 2A and A² for sufficiently large U, uniformly in the bin. The upper gap in exponent is at least λ−O(1/log U), and the lower gap is at least t₀−m−4λ−O(1/log U). Apply the exact smooth truncation and common-frequency separation of [the long-block proof](long-blocks.md). It gives the three saturated factors x₁,x₂,y₁ with x₁+x₂+y₁=r+O(1/log U) and each xᵢ≤t₀−2λ+O(1/log U)<t₀−λ. Keep at most T₁/16 for each of the outer and inner separation shifts, both measured from the originally chosen γ, and reserve the remaining common height allowance for the subsequent pointwise Mellin integrals. This preserves the same presentation, original Euler masks and row-independent physical prime coefficients. Remove any rows on which x₁ or x₂ is at least b+λ or y₁ is at least m+λ. Single-factor counts apply to these descendants directly through their available saturated factor and prime amplitudes. Their proof does not require these descendant lengths to be witness lengths at t₀.

The four upper bounds now hold simultaneously. Their sum is at least 2t₀−5λ−O(1/log U). Since 2t₀=2b+2m, subtracting the other three upper bounds gives each factor its respective lower bound b−8λ−O(1/log U) or m−8λ−O(1/log U). Absorb the fixed support errors into λ by increasing the lower threshold for U. **A sufficient two-inverse estimate.** On the remaining common fibres put s=x₁+x₂. The rational replay gives

    s>1.50,     63/50+1/250 < R+δs < 32/25.

Thus a uniform second moment for the product of the two actual inverse blocks of size O(U^(63/50+ε)), with the preceding profiles, masks and rowwise norm heights, would bound the remaining rows below R−η. It suffices to prove it on the four-factor fibres. Min and max over P are used solely to obtain this explicit sufficient rational price.

The canonical sextic inverse estimate in the preprint has one inverse block accompanied by prime slots.
