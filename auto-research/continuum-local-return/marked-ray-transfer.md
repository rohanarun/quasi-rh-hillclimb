## Centered and positive exceptional rows

Keep the original signed subtraction on the native exceptional rows. Freeze the row and its surviving prime labels. The two induced ordinary characters $\vartheta_i$ belong to finite fixed families. Before annular Mellin inversion the good unrestricted primes contribute the two Hecke factors times the correction
$$
 H_3(X,Y)=(1-X)(1-Y)(1+X+Y+X^2+XY+Y^2)=1+O((|X|+|Y|)^3),
 \quad X=\vartheta_1(p)(Np)^{-s_1},\quad Y=\vartheta_2(p)(Np)^{-s_2}.
$$
The Euler correction is absolutely bounded when both real parts are at least $2/5$. At a prime of the frozen puncture or a frozen mark use its actual residual polynomial instead. For a puncture of polynomial norm the absolute contribution of these local changes is $U^\epsilon$, since the local upper bounds there are $1+O((Np)^{-2/5})$. At a prescribed mark prime its extracted coefficient and normalization are already outside the residual sum. There is no need to divide by $H_3$ at a punctured prime.

Shift each Mellin contour from $1+\epsilon'$ to $2/5$. The two profiles give rapid vertical decay and a fixed polynomial in the incoming heights. For divided scales $T_1,T_2\ge1$, apart from the common residue, the raw bound is
