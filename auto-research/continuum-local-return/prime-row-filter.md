# A prime filter for the complete physical row sum

Assume the preprint's $7/8$ theorem and tracker #143's admitted native moment. A single nonnegative weight on **all Eisenstein elements** majorizes the surviving physical rows up to a fixed factor and vanishes on every row excluded from the native initial energy. It gives an all-element Poisson kernel for the original marked columns and allows shortened admissible products to be estimated against the very same measure.

## A uniform polylogarithmic prime test

Write $q_p=Np$, $q_z=|z|^2$, and freeze the physical parameters, original exclusions $S$, source character $\eta=\mathrm{fixedSourcePrincipal}(S)$ and fixed ideal $\mathfrak q=\mathrm{internalQ}(Q,\eta)$. Take amplifier primes outside all these fixed primes in the identity class of a fixed ray modulo a multiple of $\mathfrak q$, $\eta$'s modulus and the original ray modulus. Only the auxiliary prime test uses this enlarged ray. Let $f\ge0$ be a fixed nonzero smooth function supported in $(1,2)$, and set
$$
 P=(\log(3U))^{32},\quad w_p=(\log q_p)f(q_p/P),\quad
 D_P=\sum_p w_p,\qquad
 A(z)=D_P^{-1}\sum_p w_p\big(1_{p\nmid z}-\Re\chi_p(z)\big).
$$
The prime sums here are on the indicated identity ray. For $z=0$ put $A(0)=0$. Each summand after removal of its scalar weight equals $\tfrac12|\chi_p(z)^6-\chi_p(z)|^2$ with the literal zero extension. Hence $0\le A(z)\le2$ on every row, and $A(v^6)=0$ including when $p\mid v$.

For a sixth-power-free physical row $q_z\ll U$ with a prime of nonzero valuation modulo six outside the fixed amplifier modulus and exclusions, the finite ray twists of $p\mapsto\chi_p(z)$ are all nonprincipal. The specified prime ramifies with local order $6/\gcd(6,v_p(z))$, and the conductor of each twist has norm $O(U)$ with a fixed-data constant. This includes all sufficiently large physical rows with $N\operatorname{rad}(z)>U^{1-10^{-5}}$. [Physical row characters and their good ramification](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-September-30-2026/build/paper.tex#L4220-L4268)

On $\sigma=15/16$ the logarithmic derivative of each primitive twist is $O(\log(2U(3+|t|)^2))$ by the global zero-free logarithmic-control lemma. For a smooth von Mangoldt sum Mellin inversion and a shift from $\Re s=2$ to $\sigma$ therefore cost $O_f(P^\sigma\log(3U))$. The Mellin transform decreases rapidly on the strip. Deleting primes of the original presentation adds $O(\log(3U))$ to its logarithmic derivative on that line, by its Euler factors and their polynomial radical. In passing to primes the higher prime powers cost $O(P^{1/2}\log^2 P)$ by ideal counting. Apply fixed ray orthogonality. For $D_P$ the principal ray character has a simple pole of $-\zeta'_K/\zeta_K$ at one of residue one. This gives $D_P\sim P\int f(x)\,dx/|\mathrm{Cl}_{\rm ray}|$ and gives the preceding error for the character-weighted prime sum. Also $\sum_{p\mid z}w_p\ll_f\log(3U)$. Since $P^{1-\sigma}=(\log(3U))^2$, this proves $A(z)=1+O(1/\log(3U))$, uniformly on the stated physical rows. In particular $A(z)\ge1/2$ eventually. A test for **exact order six** also works on the large-radical class. There is a prime outside the fixed set with valuation one, since otherwise $N\operatorname{rad}(z)\ll_S q_z^{1/2}$. For each $1\le j\le5$ the corresponding $j$-th power character remains ramified there. The preceding estimate for each power and finite Fourier expansion on $\mu_6$ give
$$
 D_P^{-1}\sum_p w_p\,1_{\operatorname{ord}(\chi_p(z))=6}
 =\tfrac13+O(1/\log(3U)).
$$
At zero the local indicator is zero, and the constant Fourier term on units uses $1_{p\nmid z}$. The simpler weight $A$ suffices below.

## Comparison with the native all-element energy

The native state keeps precisely $z\ne0$ outside $\mathrm{FixedInducingRow}(\eta,\mathfrak q,m,1,z)$, where $m=\mathrm{fixedBadMask}\cdot\mathrm{idealGenerator}(1)$. The excluded condition supplies a presentation $\chi$ induced by a primitive $\psi$ of conductor dividing $\mathfrak q$, with element coefficients equal to the literal source row twist. On any amplifier prime $p\nmid z$, choose a generator congruent to one modulo the fixed ray, which is possible in its identity ray class. At $p$ the factor from $\eta$ is one, and $m^6$ contributes one since $p\nmid m$. The element coefficient of the row is therefore the nonzero $\chi_p(z)$. Hence the presentation is a unit at $p$ and its value equals $\psi(p)=1$. For $p\mid z$ the local filter is already zero. This proves
$$
 0\le A(z)\le 2\,1_{\mathrm{initialKeep}(\eta,\mathfrak q,z)}
 \quad(z\in\mathcal O).
$$
The order-six test also vanishes on every excluded row by this proof. ## The complete shifted kernel

Let $\lambda_c$ be the physical marked coefficients of the two ordinary and selected prime factors. Write $K^0_V(c,c')=\sum_{z\in\mathcal O} W_{\rm row}(q_z/V)\chi_c(z)\overline{\chi_{c'}(z)}$. Each physical positive moment on the shell is bounded by a fixed multiple of the full $A$-weighted moment, when $W_{\rm row}$ majorizes that shell. The latter has the **exact** kernel
$$
 D_P^{-1}\sum_p w_p\big[
 K^0_V(pc,pc')-\tfrac12K^0_V(pc,c')-\tfrac12K^0_V(c,pc')\big].
$$
This uses $\chi_p\overline{\chi_p}=1_{p\nmid z}$ and works also when $p\mid cc'$. Each shifted pair $(l,l')$ has primitive element conductor $d=\prod_{r:\,v_r(l)-v_r(l')\not\equiv0\ (6)}r$ and separate mask $E=\operatorname{rad}(ll')/d$. Expand the mask by $e\mid E$; after writing $z=e_0 k$ with a fixed generator $e_0$, the radial scale is $Y=V/Ne$. For $d>1$ primitive Poisson has zero constant term, with every original nonunit zero at $d$ retained. For $d=1$ use the principal row count and the sixth-equivalent column-pair bound. The average weights sum to one. Reindexing a column by multiplication by $p$ is injective and enlarges the normalized divisor envelope by at most $(2P)^{1/2}$ on that side. Hence every cost from these shifts is $U^{o(1)}$. The residual signed condition must use this same three-term kernel. For example, for columns of norm at most $U^{.926}$ the $O_\epsilon(U^{1+\epsilon})$ absolute-pair sector now covers $Nd\le U$ or $Nd_6\le U^{1537/1625}$, where $d_6$ consists of the local order-six primes. One may use original-pair cutoffs since one shift changes either product by at most $2P$ in either direction. To see the enlargement, use the box estimate of the complete-conductor proof without its count of sixth-power row divisors. In its notation the exponent is $y/2+s/2+(\phi(a,b)-a)/2$, with $a\ge s/2$, $s=\log_U Nd_6$, $D=\log_U Nd$, $y=\log_U Y$ and $b=\max(0,\min(y,D-y))$. The $D\le1$ comparison follows from the earlier upper bound. For $1\le D\le463/250+o(1)$ the four terms after substituting $a=s/2$ attain their maxima at $y=1$, allowing ties. They are
$$
 \tfrac12+\tfrac s2,\quad \tfrac D2+\tfrac s4,\quad
 \tfrac13+\tfrac D6+\tfrac{11s}{24},\quad
 \tfrac1{12}+\tfrac{5D}{12}+\tfrac s3 .
$$
Since $d^2/d_6\mid ll'$, one has $D\le(463/250+s)/2+o(1)$. At $s=1537/1625$ the four bounds are $1581/1625,12167/13000,1,1963/2000$, and each is increasing in $s$. Fixed dyadic factors, bounded scales and prime shifts fit in the prescribed $\epsilon$. The native marked moment removes further pairs with large square parts or a large common divisor. For the pairs that survive, the signed kernel above leads to explicit bounds on the dual frequencies.
