# Repeated rational primes in the signed contact detector

Assume the preprint’s $7/8$ theorem and the buffered pointwise and marked estimates of tracker #143. Fix a row in the original critical amplitude class and a dyad of the signed cross term $A\overline B$ at total scale $Z=U^\ell$. Its two ideal columns are $ln$ and $cd$, with Möbius coefficients on $l,c$, ordinary coefficients on $n,d$, and the original zero-extended character $\psi$ and its conjugate on the respective sides. Let $D$ be the product of the full prime powers $p^e\Vert N(lncd)$ with $e\ge2$, also including the entire power at every rational prime below the fixed exclusions. For each fixed $\xi>0$ the signed contribution from $D\ge U^\xi$ on this row is

$$
 O_\epsilon\bigl(Z^\delta U^{-\delta\xi/2+\epsilon}\bigr).
$$

This includes each of the three total-weight tests used in the signed estimate. For $\xi=2\cdot10^{-6}$ the per-row saving is at least $4.25\cdot10^{-7}$. Summing over the class of size $O_\epsilon(U^{R+\epsilon})$ meets even the signed price $U^{R-2\eta}Z^\delta$, where $\eta=10^{-7}$. In the remaining pairs the product of the good rational primes occurring exactly once in $N(lncd)$ is $\gg Z^2U^{-\xi}$. Every such prime splits, and the one prime ideal used has local order six in the paired conductor. The signed estimate on this complementary set remains open. ## Extraction of the repeated primes

At each rational prime included in $D$, extract its entire place-supported part from each of the four ideal indices. Freeze the resulting ideal tuple $(d_1,d_2,d_3,d_4)$ with $\prod_i Nd_i=D$. The four residual ideals have jointly squarefree rational norm product, coprime to the rational support of the frozen tuple and the fixed exclusions. These conditions recover precisely the tuples with the specified extracted parts. Omit empty terms and terms with a nonsquarefree extracted inverse ideal. Coprimality then factors the two Möbius signs exactly. The frozen character factors have modulus at most one, and all four residual coefficients retain their true phases and zeros.

Write $Y_i$ for the original four dyadic scales, so $\prod_i Y_i\asymp Z^2$. Replacing the original normalization $1/Z$ by $\prod_iY_i^{-1/2}$ changes it by a bounded factor. Extraction contributes $D^{-1/2}$ and leaves normalized dyads at $Y'_i=Y_i/Nd_i$. A nonempty center below one is bounded away from zero by the fixed annular support and can be recentered at a bounded scale. Separate the two coupled total tests by Fourier inversion in their log norms. After rescaling, their arguments on the four shortened annuli have the original bounded scale ratios. Thus the fixed seminorms are uniform in the extracted ideals, including for the tests multiplied by log powers. The original common norm twists give individual norm phases on the shortened indices and a constant of modulus one. Choose sufficiently large fixed Fourier tail orders after fixing the original height shares. [Fourier separation on the signed dyads](signed-detector-cross-term.md#definition-and-localization)

## The joint squarefree restriction

Let $F$ be the product of the four unrestricted label series in separate ideal variables, with the original two ordinary and two inverse coefficients. Let $G$ restrict these tuples to jointly squarefree rational norm and exclude the extracted and fixed primes. At a split good prime $p$ outside the extracted support, put $\epsilon_i=1$ on ordinary labels and $-1$ on inverse labels. With one variable $X_{i,\mathfrak p}$ per label and prime ideal above $p$, the local factors are

$$G_p=1+\sum_{i,\mathfrak p\mid p}\epsilon_i X_{i,\mathfrak p},\qquad F_p=\prod_{i,\mathfrak p\mid p}(1-X_{i,\mathfrak p})^{-\epsilon_i}.$$

The linear terms of $G_p/F_p$ vanish. At any fixed $\sigma'>1/2$ its absolute coefficient norm, with weight $(N\mathfrak p)^{-\sigma'}$ per variable, is $1+O(p^{-2\sigma'})$ outside a fixed finite set. Evaluation at the original local character values, including zeros, preserves this upper bound. At inert primes $G_p=1$ and every nonconstant monomial already has rational norm at least $p^2$. At an extracted prime $G_p=1$, and the absolute norm of $1/F_p$ is at most $(1-p^{-\sigma'})^{-8}$. Their product costs $O_\epsilon(U^\epsilon)$ since $D$ has polynomial size in $U$. The finitely many other fixed factors have absolutely convergent local ratios. It follows that the kernel $G/F$ has absolute coefficient norm $O_\epsilon(U^\epsilon)$ at $\sigma'$, uniformly in the row and the frozen tuple. [Multivariate rational-prime Euler factors](rational-short-inverse.md)

Convolve this kernel with the four original label series. A divisor tuple of total norm $Q$ costs an additional $Q^{-1/2}$ in the central normalizations. At $a=(1+\delta)/2$, the buffered bounds for the four further shortened dyads give $O_\epsilon(U^\epsilon(\prod_iY'_i/Q)^{a-1/2})$ on occupied annuli. The ordinary estimates allow the unreflected bound throughout this fixed length range. Taking $\sigma'=a$ in the kernel and restoring $D^{-1/2}$ gives $O_\epsilon(Z^\delta U^\epsilon D^{-a})$ for the whole frozen tuple. This step sums the residual indices with their signs. [Buffered ordinary and inverse bounds](long-blocks.md)

For each integer $D$ the number of frozen tuples is divisor-bounded, since its local exponents distribute among at most eight labels and places. Outside the fixed exclusions $D$ is powerful. The sum over frozen tuples of $D^{-1/2-\varepsilon'}$ converges for every $\varepsilon'>0$, since each good Euler factor is $1+O_{\varepsilon'}(p^{-1-2\varepsilon'})$. At $D\ge U^\xi$, Rankin’s inequality therefore saves $U^{-\xi(a-1/2-\varepsilon')}$. Choose $\varepsilon'$ and the buffered losses to obtain the stated estimate, also after summing logarithmically many dyads. Any further condition depending only on the frozen tuple can be retained.

A smooth cutoff on the norm $N\mathfrak q_6$ of the product of the paired conductor primes of exact local order six works as well. For a frozen tuple, $N\mathfrak q_6$ is a fixed factor from the extracted ratios times the product of the four residual norms. A fixed smooth transition in $\log(N\mathfrak q_6/(Z^2U^{-\xi}))$ separates by one additional Fourier frequency with uniform fixed seminorms on the annuli. Its support on $N\mathfrak q_6\ll Z^2 U^{-\xi}$ forces $D\gg U^\xi$. This proves the same bound for this smooth conductor cutoff. The initial criterion in terms of $D$ uses an exact threshold.
